How It Works

A simple daily rhythm, built for working teachers — classes are in the evening, after school hours.

1

Join the Live Class

Online classes run Monday to Friday in the evening, after school hours. Mark your attendance right from your dashboard.

2

Do the Daily Task

Pick the day's task in your dashboard. The Help guide shows exactly what to do and how your reviewer will check it.

3

Publish & Submit Links

Post your work publicly on Facebook, YouTube or LinkedIn, then submit the links with your post description.

4

Get Reviewed & Certified

Your facilitator approves your tasks one by one. Reach 100% of a level and your certificate unlocks.
